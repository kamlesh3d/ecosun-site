About EcoSun Energy

EcoSun Energy is a registered renewable energy and rooftop solar Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) enterprise headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Led by Proprietor Kamlesh Kushwah, we specialize in designing, supplying, and commissioning high-efficiency residential, housing society, commercial, and industrial solar power plants.

We facilitate end-to-end execution under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, including 3D roof shadow analysis, structural fabrication, MPEB net-metering approvals, and direct bank subsidy disbursement—using MNRE/ALMM-approved solar modules from Adani Solar, Waaree, Tata Power Solar, and Luminous.
